Research
--------

.. container:: sec-link

  `All eight themes → </research/>`_

Research spans several connected areas of computational RNA biology, linked by
a common focus on RNA structure, dynamics, evolution, and function.

.. container:: cards

  .. container:: blueprint

    .. container:: card-h

      Structure & kinetics

    Secondary structure ensembles, folding pathways, and kinetic models, from
    ViennaRNA based analysis to machine learned folding dynamics.

  .. container:: blueprint

    .. container:: card-h

      Viral RNA biology

    Conserved RNA elements, structured viral genomes, comparative genomics, and
    phylodynamic analysis across emerging and medically relevant viruses.

  .. container:: blueprint

    .. container:: card-h

      RNA design & computational methods

    Computational design and analysis of functional RNAs, sequence and structure
    relationships, reproducible workflows, and data driven methods.

Michael T. Wolfinger
--------------------

.. container:: kicker

  About

.. container:: portrait

  .. figure:: /static/images/mtw.jpg
    :figclass: blueprint
    :alt: Michael T. Wolfinger

  .. container:: portrait-main

    Computational RNA biologist. His research covers RNA structure prediction and folding kinetics, structure-aware RNA design, RNA-protein recognition and structured viral RNAs. Much of it centres on RNAs whose function comes from their structure, not just their sequence.

    He is an independent Principal Investigator, leading research projects as well as collaborative projects with the Theoretical Biochemistry Group at the University of Vienna, a long-standing centre of RNA bioinformatics and home of the ViennaRNA Package. In 2023 and 2024 he was a visiting professor at the University of Freiburg.

    He pursues his independent research through RNA Forecast, his platform for research, software development, scientific collaborations, and teaching activities.
