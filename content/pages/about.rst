Biography
---------

.. container:: split-wide

  Michael T. Wolfinger has worked in computational RNA biology for more than two decades. He is an independent Principal Investigator and maintains long-standing research collaborations with the Theoretical Biochemistry Group at the University of Vienna, a major centre of RNA bioinformatics and home of the ViennaRNA Package.

  In 2023 and 2024 he served as a visiting professor at the University of Freiburg, where he worked on machine learning approaches for RNA folding kinetics and computational RNA biology. His current work combines independently led projects through RNA Forecast with collaborative research involving academic groups in Austria and internationally.

Research interests
------------------

Michael's research spans computational approaches to RNA structure, dynamics, evolution, and design, with particular interests in functional RNAs and structured viral RNAs. Current research themes include:

Teaching & advanced training
----------------------------

.. container:: split-wide

  Michael has taught computational biology, bioinformatics and RNA analysis at universities in Austria and Germany for more than twenty years, from graduate courses to invited lectures. A fuller record is kept in the `teaching portfolio <https://michaelwolfinger.com/teaching/>`__ on his personal site.

  Through RNA Forecast, he also develops and delivers advanced workshops on the ViennaRNA ecosystem and computational RNA analysis for academic and industrial groups, drawing directly on methods and workflows used in his research.
