What is processed, and on what basis
------------------------------------

**Server logs.** This site is hosted on GitHub Pages and delivered through
Cloudflare, which sits in front of it as a content delivery network and
terminates the encrypted connection. Both record technical access data,
including your IP address, in order to deliver the site and to protect it
against misuse. Legal basis: Article 6(1)(f) GDPR, the legitimate interest in
operating a secure website.

**Audience measurement.** Cloudflare Web Analytics counts visits. A small
script from Cloudflare, added to each page as it is delivered, reports the page
you viewed, the page that referred you, your approximate location by country,
your browser and device type, and how the page performed in your browser,
including the loading and responsiveness measurements known as Core Web Vitals.
It sets no cookie, neither writes to nor reads from your device's storage, and
creates no identifier that follows you between sites or between visits, so no
profile is built and you are not recognised when you come back. Legal basis:
Article 6(1)(f) GDPR, the legitimate interest in knowing which parts of the
site are read. Because nothing is stored on or read from your device,
§ 165 TKG 2021 does not apply and this measurement runs without consent; you
may object to it under Article 21 GDPR.

**Google Analytics, only with your consent.** On your first visit a dialog
asks whether Google Analytics 4, a service of Google Ireland Limited, Gordon
House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland, may measure how the site is used.
Before you accept, no connection to Google is made and nothing from Google is
loaded. After you accept, Google Analytics sets the cookies ``_ga`` and
``_ga_*`` on your device, which last up to two years, and records pseudonymous
usage data: the pages you view, when and for how long, the page that referred
you, your approximate location derived from your IP address, which Google
Analytics 4 does not store in full, and your device, browser and screen. The
purpose is audience measurement and nothing else; the data is not used for
advertising, and Google Signals is switched off. Legal basis: Article 6(1)(a)
GDPR, your consent, together with § 165(3) TKG 2021 for the storage of
information on your device. Google may transfer data to Google LLC in the
United States; Google LLC is certified under the EU–US Data Privacy Framework,
and the European Commission's standard contractual clauses in Google's data
processing terms apply in addition. Event-level data is kept for 14 months and
thereafter only in aggregate. Google describes its own processing at
`policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
<https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites>`__ and
`policies.google.com/privacy <https://policies.google.com/privacy>`__.

**Your consent choice.** Whether you accepted or declined is stored in your
browser's local storage under the key ``rnaf-consent`` for twelve months, so
that the dialog does not reappear on every visit. Declining has no effect on
your use of the site. If your browser sends a Global Privacy Control signal,
the dialog is not shown and Google Analytics stays off. You can change or
withdraw your choice at any time through the “Cookie settings” link in the
footer; withdrawal takes effect for the future and does not affect the
lawfulness of processing carried out beforehand. Storing that choice is
necessary to honour it, so it needs no consent of its own. Legal basis:
Article 6(1)(c) and (f) GDPR.

**Contact form.** The form is delivered by FormSubmit, which forwards the name,
email address and message you enter to RNA Forecast by email. Providing those
details is necessary if you want a reply through the form; you can write
directly to the email address above instead. Legal basis: Article 6(1)(b) GDPR
where your enquiry concerns a possible collaboration, otherwise
Article 6(1)(f) GDPR, the legitimate interest in answering enquiries.

**Direct email.** If you write directly, your message and address are used only
to reply to you. Legal basis: Article 6(1)(f) GDPR.

No automated decision-making or profiling within the meaning of Article 22 GDPR
takes place on this site.

Recipients and transfers outside the EU
---------------------------------------

The processors named above — GitHub, Cloudflare, Google and FormSubmit — act
on behalf of RNA Forecast, and processing may involve transfers to the United
States.
Such transfers are made on the basis of the European Commission's
adequacy decision for the EU–US Data Privacy Framework where the recipient is
certified under it, and otherwise on the basis of the European Commission's
standard contractual clauses. Data is not sold, and is not passed to anyone
else except where the law requires it.

Retention
---------

Enquiries received by email or through the contact form are kept for as long as
needed to deal with the matter, and thereafter only where a statutory retention
period applies. Access logs are kept for the retention period of the hosting
and delivery services, which is short and measured in days rather than years.
The aggregate visit figures held by Cloudflare Web Analytics are kept for that
service's own retention period. Google Analytics event data is kept for
14 months, as set out above, and the record of your consent choice for twelve
months in your own browser.

Your rights
-----------

You have the right to obtain confirmation of whether your data is processed and
to access it (Article 15 GDPR), to have inaccurate data corrected
(Article 16 GDPR), to have data erased (Article 17 GDPR), to have processing
restricted (Article 18 GDPR), to receive data you provided in a portable form
(Article 20 GDPR), and to object at any time to processing based on legitimate
interests (Article 21 GDPR).

Where processing rests on your consent, you may withdraw it at any time with
effect for the future, and withdrawing is as easy as giving it: open
“Cookie settings” in the footer and change your choice. The Google Analytics
cookies already set can be deleted in your browser.

To exercise any of these rights, write to the email address above.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. The
authority responsible for Austria is:
